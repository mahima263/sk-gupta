About the Initiative

For more than six decades, IIT Kanpur has stood at the forefront of education, research, and innovation shaped by the brilliance of its faculty and students. As part of its long-standing commitment to Faculty Development Initiatives, the Institute continually invests in supporting educators who define its academic strength and national impact.

It is in this spirit, we thank the Class of 1986, for helping the Institute to establish the Prof. S.K. Gupta Faculty Chair in tribute to Prof. Santosh K. Gupta, whose exemplary scholarship, mentorship, and service embody the highest ideals of academic leadership at IIT Kanpur. IIT Kanpur believes our faculty are visionaries who inspire students, advance the boundaries of knowledge, and strengthen India's research ecosystem.

Prof. S.K. Gupta Faculty Chair stands as a distinguished recognition of academic excellence, created to support and empower senior faculty members who demonstrate leadership in teaching, research, and institutional contribution, reflecting the values and impact exemplified by Prof. Gupta.

Honoring Prof. Santosh K. Gupta

President's Gold Medalist 1968

A Distinguished Voice in Chemical Engineering

Prof. Santosh K. Gupta, President's Gold Medal from the IITK batch of 1968, is one of the most distinguished voices in Chemical Engineering in India and across the world. He re-joined IIT Kanpur as faculty in 1973, became a full Professor in 1980, and dedicated nearly four decades to advancing Polymer Reaction Engineering, Optimization, and Process Systems Engineering.

His scholarly impact is immense. His books such as Optimization for Engineers, Numerical Methods for Engineers, and Mathematical Methods in Chemical and Environmental Engineering are widely used across universities and industries worldwide.

Prof. Gupta's career is adorned with Fellowships at INAE, NASI, and IISc Academies, as well as the IIT Kanpur Institute Fellowship 2021. Yet his deepest legacy lies in his extraordinary commitment to mentorship, a quality that shaped generations of students.

About the Faculty Chair

Prof. S.K. Gupta Faculty Chair is being instituted to recognize and support outstanding academic leadership in the field of Mechanical Engineering at IIT Kanpur. The Chair aims to attract, retain, and empower distinguished faculty members who demonstrate excellence in teaching, research, and institutional contribution.

By providing dedicated support, the Chair enables the incumbent to pursue high-impact research, mentor students, and contribute meaningfully to the academic and innovation ecosystem of the Institute.
